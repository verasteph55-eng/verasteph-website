Who we are

A cooperative approach to inclusive agribusiness

Verasteph is a Nigerian cooperative organisation focused on agricultural produce aggregation, rural livelihood improvement, and more inclusive food systems.

We seek to connect farmers, buyers, and value-chain stakeholders through collective marketing, coordinated produce collection, and stronger commercial relationships. Our approach is designed to help address fragmented markets, high transportation costs, limited bargaining power, and challenges reaching reliable buyers.

Geographic focus

Our stated operational focus includes Benue, Nasarawa, Kogi, and Plateau States, as well as the Federal Capital Territory (FCT).

Produce categories

Yam, rice, beans, cassava, garri, soybeans, onions, tomatoes, sweet potatoes, grains, vegetables, and fruits.

Our development priorities

Inclusive growth with practical outcomes

We want agricultural value chains to work better for producers and the communities that depend on them.

  • Improve smallholder farmers' access to reliable markets and commercial opportunities.
  • Strengthen aggregation, storage, transportation, and distribution systems.
  • Expand economic opportunities for women and young people.
  • Support rural employment, entrepreneurship, and income diversification.
  • Contribute to food security and more efficient handling of agricultural produce.
  • Build partnerships that can deliver measurable and sustainable outcomes.

We are committed to reporting results transparently. Specific beneficiary counts, performance figures, and project outcomes will be published when verified.
